Privacy Policy

Nicholls Home Automation is a personal, non-commercial app used only by its owner to connect their own Google Nest devices to their own Home Assistant server.

The app does not collect, sell, share or publish any user data. Account access tokens are stored only on the owner's own server inside their home. No data is sent to any third party by this app, and no one other than the owner uses it.

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
